PhD in Physics | Photonics Researcher | Nanophotonics | Hybrid Photonic Nanostructures

I am a Physics researcher with a PhD from Friedrich Schiller University Jena, Germany. My doctoral research focused on: **"Creation and Investigation of Hybrid-Photonic Nanostructures with TMDs"**. <br>
My research interests lie at the intersection of photonics, nanotechnology, and advanced functional materials, with particular emphasis on hybrid photonic nanostructures, transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs), and light–matter interactions. I have experience in electromagnetic simulations, optical characterization, spectroscopy, and nanofabrication. I enjoy combining computational modelling and experimental techniques to investigate photonic structures and explore their optical properties.
